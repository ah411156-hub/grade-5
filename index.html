5
Pool safety first!

We must stay safe! 🛟

Must means it is important to do. Mustn’t means do not do it.

🚶We must walk.
🛟We must listen to the lifeguard.
🚿We must shower first.
🏃We mustn’t run.
🙅We mustn’t push.
👫We mustn’t swim alone.
MayaWe must stay with our grown-up, too!
LeoGood idea. Safety makes swimming fun! 💦
6
Write to a friend

A friendly email 📧

A friendly email has a greeting, a message, and a sign-off.

✉️ To: leo@example.com   •   Subject: Saturday fun!
Hi Leo,
Would you like to go to the swimming pool on Saturday? We also have a new playground there. I like swimming, too!
Write back soon.
Your friend,
Maya

also usually goes before the action: “I also have…”   •   too often goes at the end: “I like it, too.”
